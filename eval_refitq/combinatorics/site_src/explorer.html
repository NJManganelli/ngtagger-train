To see what a score buys, put it on an axis and pick a resolution as the metric: that is sigma(parameter) against score. For the joint distribution instead, put the score on one axis and a residual on the other with "tracks per bin".

"Tracks shown" are the candidates surviving duplicate removal, the cuts and the score threshold. Every track panel bins fitted quantities; the efficiency panel bins truth.

Resolutions are residuals against the TrackingParticle, not the fit's own covariance, which is measured 1.6-3.4x optimistic even on clean tracks. Truth is referenced to the particle owning the most hits on the track, so the residual and the wrong-hit count describe the same particle.

Seed-truthful: the seed's own clusters all belong to the particle. It judges a seed — could this pair of layers start a search that reaches this particle — is fixed when the census is built, and does not move when you change the cuts or the score thresholds.
Delivered: some surviving track is owned by the particle under the rule above. It judges the system — does a usable track come out — and responds to the seeds, duplicate removal, cuts and scores.
They differ because the search adds hits after seeding and sometimes adds the wrong ones, so a track can start on one particle and end up made of another's hits. Seed-truthful is the more generous number.
